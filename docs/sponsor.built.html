档位Tiers

按「有多少人真的用起来」分档Priced by how much real usage you get

黑客松能卖的、别的赞助形式给不了的东西只有一样：把「基于你的产品构建」写进参赛规则。所以档位不按 logo 大小分，按使用保障和排他性分。There's one thing a hackathon can sell that no other format can: writing "build on this product" into the rules. So the ladder is graded by usage guarantee and exclusivity, not logo size.

最高档 · 买断整场Top tier · the whole event

独家专场Exclusive Build

不是「最大的 logo」，是这场黑客松为你而办。主题、赛题、日期跟着你的产品发布节奏走，全场不出现任何其他品牌。对一个刚发新 SDK 的团队来说，这等于让一屋子开发者在同一天集中压测你的新 API，并留下一批可直接发布的 demo。Not "the biggest logo" — the entire hackathon is built for you. Theme, challenges and date follow your launch calendar, and no other brand appears anywhere. For a team that just shipped an SDK, this is a room of developers stress-testing your new API in a single day, and a set of demos you can publish afterwards.

FAQ

常见问题Questions we always get

截止时间是什么时候？What's the deadline?

主视觉与印刷物料需在活动前两周定稿，logo 与冠名信息须在此之前确认。确切日期：待定Key art and printed collateral lock two weeks before the event; logos and naming must be confirmed by then. Exact date: TBC

可以只出算力或奖品，不出现金吗？Can we sponsor in credits or prizes instead of cash?

可以。实物赞助按折算价值对应到相应档位权益，算力、API credits、工具授权、餐饮、奖品都收。Yes. In-kind support is assessed by value and mapped to the equivalent tier. Compute, API credits, tool licenses, catering and prizes all work.

能拿到参会者名单吗？Do we get the attendee list?

独家、冠名与产品档拿到 opt-in 名单，即报名时明确勾选同意被赞助方联系的那部分参会者。我们不提供全量名单，任何档位都不提供。Exclusive, Title and Build-on sponsors receive the opt-in list — attendees who ticked the box agreeing to be contacted. We don't hand over the full list, at any tier.

我们可以自己出赛题吗？Can we set our own challenge?

可以，产品档及以上都能出题。你定题目和评判标准，我们把它写进参赛规则——这正是你花的钱买到的东西：不是希望有人用你的产品，是规则要求他们用。Yes — Build-on and above. You write the brief and the judging criteria, and we put it in the rules. That's the thing you're actually paying for: not hoping someone uses your product, but a rule that requires it.

价格为什么不写在页面上？Why isn't pricing on this page?

档位和加购的组合差别很大，同一档对不同公司的价值也不一样——直接报一个数字对双方都不划算。约 15 分钟，我们按你想要的结果算给你听。付款方式与开票在这一步一并确认。Tier and add-on combinations vary a lot, and the same tier is worth very different things to different companies — a single posted number serves neither of us. Give us 15 minutes and we'll price it against the outcome you're after. Payment terms and invoicing get settled in the same conversation.

排他性具体怎么算？How does exclusivity actually work?

按品类划分，写进合同。独家专场全品类排他，全场不出现任何其他品牌；冠名档锁住同品类竞品，本场不得以任何档位进入；产品档同品类最多 2 家；社区档不排他。品类边界在签约前双方书面确认，避免事后扯皮。By category, written into the contract. Exclusive Build locks out every other brand entirely; Title locks out direct competitors from entering at any tier; Build-on caps a category at two companies; Community carries no exclusivity. We agree the category boundary in writing before signing, so there's nothing to argue about later.

活动之后有什么？What happens after the event?

活动全程有摄制，剪辑成片会在我们的社媒渠道发布，赞助方在片中露出。往期活动的现场素材可按需提供。The event is filmed and edited into content published on our channels, with sponsors featured. Footage from past events is available on request.
