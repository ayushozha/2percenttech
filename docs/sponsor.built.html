FAQ

常见问题Questions we always get

截止时间是什么时候？What's the deadline?

主视觉与印刷物料需在活动前两周定稿，logo 与冠名信息须在此之前确认。确切日期：待定Key art and printed collateral lock two weeks before the event; logos and naming must be confirmed by then. Exact date: TBC

能拿到参会者名单吗？Do we get the attendee list?

三个套餐都拿到 opt-in 名单，即报名时明确勾选同意被赞助方联系的那部分参会者。我们不提供全量名单，任何套餐都不提供。All three packages include the opt-in list — attendees who ticked the box agreeing to be contacted. We don't hand over the full list, under any package.

我们可以自己出赛题吗？Can we set our own challenge?

可以，三个套餐都能出题。联合主办含一个品牌赛题；独家与旗舰专场的主题、赛题、规则与议程整套由你参与设计。你定题目和评判标准，我们把它写进参赛规则——这正是你花的钱买到的东西：不是希望有人用你的产品，是规则要求他们用。Yes, under all three packages. Co-Hosted includes one branded challenge; Exclusive and Flagship cover the whole theme, challenge, rules and agenda. You write the brief and the judging criteria, and we put it in the rules. That's the thing you're actually paying for: not hoping someone uses your product, but a rule that requires it.

价格为什么不写在页面上？Why isn't pricing on this page?

档位和加购的组合差别很大，同一档对不同公司的价值也不一样——直接报一个数字对双方都不划算。约 15 分钟，我们按你想要的结果算给你听。付款方式与开票在这一步一并确认。Tier and add-on combinations vary a lot, and the same tier is worth very different things to different companies — a single posted number serves neither of us. Give us 15 minutes and we'll price it against the outcome you're after. Payment terms and invoicing get settled in the same conversation.

排他性具体怎么算？How does exclusivity actually work?

按品类划分，写进合同。品牌独家专场与旗舰专场全程不出现同品类竞品；联合主办为共享形式，不含排他。品类边界在签约前双方书面确认，避免事后扯皮。By category, written into the contract. The Exclusive Brand and Flagship packages carry no same-category competing sponsor; Co-Hosted is a shared format and carries no exclusivity. We agree the category boundary in writing before signing, so there's nothing to argue about later.

活动之后有什么？What happens after the event?

活动全程有摄制，剪辑成片会在我们的社媒渠道发布，赞助方在片中露出。往期活动的现场素材可按需提供。The event is filmed and edited into content published on our channels, with sponsors featured. Footage from past events is available on request.
